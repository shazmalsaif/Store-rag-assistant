## 1. PulseCore X1 Wireless Headphones

**Category:** Audio / Over-Ear Headphones

The PulseCore X1 is a closed-back wireless headphone designed for music, gaming, video calls, and everyday travel. Its 40 mm dynamic drivers deliver a balanced sound profile with enhanced low-frequency response, while hybrid active noise cancellation reduces surrounding environmental noise.

### Technical Specifications

* **Dimensions:** 19.2 × 17.1 × 8.1 cm
* **Weight:** 248 g
* **Materials:** ABS polymer housing, stainless-steel headband, protein-leather ear cushions
* **Driver:** 40 mm dynamic
* **Frequency response:** 20 Hz–20 kHz
* **Battery:** 600 mAh lithium-ion
* **Battery life:** Up to 38 hours; up to 28 hours with ANC enabled
* **Charging:** USB-C, approximately 2 hours
* **Connectivity:** Bluetooth 5.3, 3.5 mm audio
* **Wireless range:** Up to 15 m
* **Microphones:** Dual beamforming microphones
* **Compatibility:** Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, ChromeOS and devices with 3.5 mm audio output

### What's Included

* PulseCore X1 headphones
* USB-C charging cable
* 3.5 mm audio cable
* Protective carrying pouch
* Quick-start guide

### Setup and Usage

1. Charge the headphones fully before first use.
2. Press and hold the power button for three seconds to enter pairing mode.
3. Select **PulseCore X1** from your device's Bluetooth menu.
4. Use the ANC button to switch between Noise Cancellation, Transparency, and Normal modes.
5. Connect the supplied 3.5 mm cable when using wired mode.

### Warranty

Fun gadgets provides a **1-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects under normal use. Physical damage, liquid damage, and unauthorized modifications are excluded.

### Care and Maintenance

Keep the headphones away from excessive moisture and direct heat. Clean cushions and surfaces with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use alcohol or abrasive cleaners. Recharge the battery at least once every three months if stored for an extended period.
