Once an order has been dispatched, customers will receive tracking information by email or through their account, where tracking is available. Tracking information may take up to 48 hours to become active after the carrier receives the package.

If tracking indicates that an order has been delivered but the customer cannot locate it, the customer should first check with household members, building reception, neighbors, or the designated delivery location. Customers should contact Fun gadgets as soon as possible if an order arrives visibly damaged, incomplete, or with incorrect items. Photographs of the package, shipping label, and damaged product may be requested to assist with the carrier claim.

Fun gadgets wants customers to be satisfied with their purchases. Eligible products may generally be returned or exchanged within **30 calendar days of confirmed delivery**, subject to the requirements in this section.

To qualify for a standard return, an item must generally be:

* In its original, resalable condition.
* Free from physical damage, excessive wear, stains, or unauthorized modifications.
* Returned with the original packaging and included accessories where reasonably possible.
* Accompanied by the order number or other reasonable proof of purchase.

Customers are responsible for taking reasonable care of products while they are in their possession. A returned product that has been damaged, modified, improperly installed, or used in a manner inconsistent with its instructions may be rejected or may receive a reduced refund where permitted by law.

Because many Fun gadgets products are electronics, certain items may not be eligible for a change-of-mind return once opened or activated. This can include products containing batteries, storage media, software licenses, personal-use components, or products where opening the packaging compromises hygiene or resaleability.

Opening a product does not automatically cancel every possible remedy. **Defective, damaged, incorrect, or materially misdescribed products may still qualify for a replacement, repair, refund, or warranty remedy regardless of whether the packaging has been opened**, subject to applicable law and the specific manufacturer's warranty.

For electronics that remain eligible for return after opening, the product must be returned with all major components, accessories, manuals, and included items. Missing accessories or significant signs of use may affect eligibility where permitted by applicable law.

### Final Sale and Non-Returnable Items

Items marked **Final Sale**, clearance products specifically identified as non-returnable, personalized or customized products, downloadable products once delivery or access has begun, and certain hygiene-sensitive or consumable items may not qualify for standard returns.

Gift cards and promotional credits are generally non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash except where required by law.

These exclusions do not limit a customer's statutory rights concerning products that are defective, unsafe, incorrectly supplied, or otherwise covered by mandatory consumer-protection laws.

### Return Process

To request a return, contact Fun gadgets customer support with your order number, the product name, reason for return, and photographs or other information where requested.

If the return is approved, Fun gadgets will provide return instructions. Customers should use the provided return address and packaging instructions. Unless the item is defective, incorrect, damaged in transit, or otherwise eligible for prepaid return shipping under our policy, the customer may be responsible for return shipping costs.

Once the returned item is received, Fun gadgets will inspect it. Approved refunds are generally processed within **5–10 business days after inspection**. The time required for funds to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable for voluntary returns unless required by law or the return resulted from a Fun gadgets error.

### Exchanges

Eligible products may be exchanged for the same product or, where available, an equivalent replacement. If the requested replacement is unavailable, Fun gadgets may offer a refund or another appropriate resolution.

# 3. Warranty Claims

Some Fun gadgets products include a manufacturer's warranty or a Fun gadgets limited warranty. The applicable warranty period is stated on the product listing, documentation, invoice, or warranty information supplied with the product.

A warranty generally covers **manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship that occur during normal and intended use**. Depending on the product and applicable warranty, the remedy may include repair, replacement, or refund.

A warranty does not normally cover damage caused by accidents, misuse, abuse, unauthorized modification, improper installation, liquid exposure where the product is not rated for it, power surges, physical impact, cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality, or normal wear and tear.

Battery capacity naturally decreases over time through normal use. Unless specifically stated otherwise, normal battery degradation is not considered a manufacturing defect.

### Filing a Warranty Claim

Customers requesting warranty service should contact Fun gadgets support and provide:

1. Order number or valid proof of purchase.
2. Product name and serial number, where applicable.
3. Description of the problem.
4. Photographs or video demonstrating the issue when requested.
5. Details of troubleshooting steps already attempted.

Fun gadgets may request additional information before approving a warranty return. In some cases, customers may be asked to perform basic troubleshooting, update firmware, reset the product, or test an alternative cable or power source.

If the claim is approved, Fun gadgets will provide instructions for returning or servicing the product. Customers should not ship a warranty item to an address other than the one provided by Fun gadgets.

Warranty claims are generally reviewed within **3–7 business days after all requested information or the returned product has been received**. Approved repairs or replacements are normally processed within **7–14 business days**, excluding shipping time and cases where replacement inventory is temporarily unavailable.

If a covered product cannot reasonably be repaired or replaced, Fun gadgets may provide a refund or another remedy permitted by applicable law.
